Temporary works engineers own the hole too: shoring for excavations, the surcharge a crane puts behind that shoring, cofferdams that hold back a river, and the pressure a mat puts on a soft layer six feet down. Most of it is Rankine, Boussinesq and buoyancy done carefully.

Apparent pressure diagrams are envelopes back-calculated from measured strut loads, not real pressure distributions. Use them for struts and wales, not for wall embedment. In soft clay, check basal heave and the stiffness and sequence of the system too. Settlement behind the wall can matter more than strength.
