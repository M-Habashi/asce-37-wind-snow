Reading list

What to read

Must

  • ASCE/SEI 37-14, Ch. 2, 4 and 6 (library or purchase)
  • Caltrans Falsework Manual, Ch. 3–5 (free)
  • FHWA NHI-15-044, Stability in Bridge Construction (free)
  • ASME BTH-1, Ch. 3 (pin-connected plates, design categories) + B30.20
  • OSHA 1926 Subpart CC (cranes) and Subpart P (excavations)
  • AISC 360: Ch. C, E, F, H, J and App. 6 (free)

Should

  • AASHTO Guide Design Specifications for Bridge Temporary Works + Construction Handbook
  • AASHTO LRFD BDS: Sec. 3 (loads, Art. 3.4.2, 3.6, 3.7) and Sec. 6 constructibility
  • ACI 347R-14, Guide to Formwork for Concrete, and ACI 347.2R (reshoring)
  • Caltrans Trenching & Shoring Manual (free)
  • AASHTO/NSBA S10.1, Steel Bridge Erection Guide Specification
  • PCI recommended practice: lateral stability of precast girders
  • WSDOT BDM Ch. 3 (an owner's take on construction loads)

Deepen

  • Ratay, Handbook of Temporary Structures in Construction
  • Hurd / ACI SP-4, Formwork for Concrete
  • AISC Design Guide 10 (erection bracing) and DG 1 (base plates)
  • CIRIA C703, Crane Stability on Site (ground bearing, mats)
  • NDS + Supplement (timber), APA Plyform guide
  • Sheet-pile design manuals, NAVFAC DM-7.02, FHWA GEC-4 (anchors)
  • Gerwick, Construction of Marine and Offshore Structures (cofferdams, barges)

Sheet 7
The other half of the interview
stories & questions

Stories to have ready

Safety call

A time you stopped or changed work because something wasn't right. Kiewit's mindset is "Nobody Gets Hurt", and its named programs include Craft Voice in Safety, Life-Saving Actions and the Operations Start Card (a hazard analysis before every operation). Be specific about what you saw, who you told and what changed.

Caught an error

A calc, drawing or assumption you checked and found wrong. Cover how you found it, how you told people, and the fix. Temporary works runs on independent checks.

Field ↔ office

Translating a design for a superintendent, or changing a design because the field had a better way. Cover means, methods and constructability.

Under schedule

A deliverable with a hard deadline (a pour, a lift window, a road closure). How did you prioritize while staying conservative?

Learning fast

A code or tool you had to learn quickly. ASCE 37 and BTH-1 from this week count.

Ask them

Which codes and owner specs govern most of their work? How is the independent check organized? What software do they use (FEA, lift planning, pile analysis)? How much field time does a new engineer get?

Built as a personal study aid. Code values were checked where a free source exists (Caltrans, WSDOT, OSHA, FHWA) and are flagged "verify" elsewhere. Always design from the current licensed standard and the project specification.
