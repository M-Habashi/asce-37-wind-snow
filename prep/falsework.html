Falsework and formwork failures usually happen during the pour. That's when the load is at its maximum, the concrete is still liquid, and the system is at its least stiff. Know the design loads cold, know the load path from soffit to soil, and know the few formulas that set the pressure.

Name the variables: rate of placement (the big one), temperature (cold concrete sets slowly, so pressure goes up), unit weight, chemistry and retarders, slump and SCC, vibration depth, and form geometry. Control the rate in the field: write the maximum pour rate on the drawings and monitor it. Hot-weather retarders can turn a 1,000 psf design into full liquid head.

Caltrans specifics worth quoting

Falsework beam deflection from the full concrete weight (treated as one pour) is limited to L/240. Earthquake isn't considered for falsework. Wind uses height-zone pressures, with a 2.2 shape factor on heavy-duty shoring. Wind generally governs once falsework is taller than about 30–40 ft; below that, the 2% load governs. Posts next to traffic need collision protection or minimum section and connection requirements. Use the owner's version: TxDOT, WSDOT and others have their own.

    Verify

    The SF 2.5 for shoring frames (SSFI practice) and the 100 plf wall-bracing minimum are from memory. Confirm them in ACI 347R and the manufacturer's literature.

    peak slab load ≈ — × its own DL

    Simplified method (ACI 347.2R idea): the new slab, forms and live load are shared equally by the N connected slabs, so each carries its own weight plus (1 + forms + LL)/N. The detailed Grundy–Kabaila analysis follows the cycle. Compare the result with the early-age capacity of the young slabs.

    OSHA 1926.703 in one breath

    Formwork drawings (including shoring and reshoring layouts) at the jobsite. Shores inspected before and during placement. Don't remove forms or shores until the concrete can carry its load, as shown by tests or specified criteria. Reshore when slabs carry construction loads before reaching design strength. Precast and lift-slab rules sit in the same subpart.
