A falsework post on a sill with nothing at the top is a flagpole with K = 2.1. Brace the top and Lc drops about 2×, so Fe rises about 4×. Design flow · §F2

Erection trap

A girder set before its cross-frames go in has Lb equal to the whole span, so erection lives on the far right of the curve. Top-flange loading is destabilizing (Cb assumes shear-center loading), and an unbraced cantilever takes Cb = 1.0. For girder pairs, check system buckling on the bridges page.

Stability bracing · AISC 360-16 Appendix 6
strength + stiffness

A brace needs strength and stiffness

Appendix 6 gives two requirements per brace, both based on an initial out-of-straightness of Lbr/500: a required strength (the old "2% rule", now 0.5–2%) and a required stiffness βbr, which is twice the ideal stiffness through the 1/φ factor. To check stiffness you compute the provided stiffness of the real brace (axial spring of the member, softened by its connections) and show it is ≥ βbr. Since 360-16 the terms are panel bracing (was "relative") and point bracing (was "nodal").

Design flow · App. 6

How to get the provided stiffness

For an axial strut or tie at angle θ to the braced movement: \(k=\frac{AE}{L}\cos^2\theta\). Springs in series add as reciprocals: \(\frac{1}{\beta_{sys}}=\frac{1}{k_{brace}}+\frac{1}{\beta_{conn}}+\dots\) (App. 6 user note to §6.2.1). Long guy cables: use the cable's effective modulus (≈ 12,000–15,000 ksi for wire rope) and pretension with a turnbuckle (FHWA NHI-15-044 §5.5). Bracing to a "rigid" support only works if that support is itself stiff, so include it in the chain.

Design flow · stability (Ch. C) → H1

Why it matters for temp works

Shoring towers, posts and spreader bars carry axial load with an accidental eccentricity: out-of-plumb, a jack off center, a sling not quite on the centroid. Near Pe that "small" eccentricity governs. The direct analysis method (§C2) models it with notional loads Ni = 0.002αYi (C2-1) and reduced stiffness 0.8τbEI, 0.8EA (§C2.3), and then lets you use Lc = L.

Steel erection rules & questions

AISC 303 §7.10

Temporary support of the frame (guys, bracing, falsework) is the erector's means and methods. The EOR must identify a frame that isn't self-supporting.

4 anchor rods

OSHA 1926.755(a): columns need at least 4 anchor rods, designed for a 300 lb eccentric load 18 in from the column face at the top.

2 bolts

OSHA 1926.756(a)(1): don't release the hoist line until each connection has at least two bolts, wrench-tight.

1 : 500

AISC 303 column plumbness tolerance. It's also the L/500 imperfection behind App. 6 and the 0.002 notional load.
