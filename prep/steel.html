Shoring legs, tower posts, spreader bars, girders before their cross-frames go in: most temporary steel is slender and lightly braced, and it hasn't got its final connections yet. The checks you learned all still apply. What changes is that out-of-plumb, unbraced length and bracing stiffness usually govern instead of yield.

Erection trap

A girder set before its cross-frames go in has Lb equal to the whole span, so the curve's far right is where erection lives. Two more things push you down the curve: load on the top flange (destabilizing, since AISC's Cb assumes shear-center loading) and a cantilever overhang (use Cb = 1.0 unless the tip is braced). Why it matters for temp works

Shoring towers, tall posts and spreader bars carry axial load with an accidental eccentricity (out-of-plumb, a jack off center, a sling not quite on the centroid). B1 = Cm/(1 − αPr/Pe1), with α = 1.0 (LRFD) or 1.6 (ASD). This amplification is why a "small" eccentricity governs once P approaches Pe. The direct analysis method (Ch. C) handles this with notional loads (0.002Yi) and reduced stiffness (0.8EI, 0.8EA).

Rehearsal

Steel erection rules & questions

AISC 303 §7.10

Temporary support of the frame (guys, bracing, falsework) is the erector's means and methods. The EOR has to identify a frame that isn't self-supporting.

4 anchor rods

OSHA 1926.755: columns need at least 4 anchor rods, designed for a 300 lb eccentric load 18 in from the column face at the top.

2 bolts

OSHA 1926.756: don't release the hoist line until each connection has at least two bolts, wrench-tight.

1 : 500

AISC 303 column plumbness tolerance. It's also the L/500 imperfection behind Appendix 6 and the 0.002 notional load.

Reading

AISC Design Guide 10, Erection Bracing of Low-Rise Structural Steel Buildings. Design Guide 1, Base Plate and Anchor Rod Design, for anchor rods during erection before grout. Manual Part 9 for prying.
