ASCE 37 doesn't replace ASCE 7. It takes the same analysis procedures, renames gravity loads by how certain they are, adds horizontal construction loads, and lowers the environmental loads to match a short exposure period. The pencil notes below mark what's new compared with your ASCE 7 course.

Two combinations do most of the work for temporary structures. The gravity combination (2) sizes members. The counteracting-dead-load combination \(0.9D+1.0W\) (ASD \(0.6D+0.6W\)) checks overturning, uplift and sliding of towers, forms and mats.

ASCE 7-22 note

ASCE 7-22 made ground snow strength-level (LRFD snow factor 1.0) and moved \(K_d\) from \(q_z\) into the pressure equations. ASCE 37-14 still points to ASCE 7-10, so construction checks use \(1.6S\) and \(q_z=0.00256K_zK_{zt}K_dV^2\) (7-10 Eq. 29.3-1). If they ask which edition, say so.

ASCE 37 vocabulary · Ch. 4 · Ch. 2
the C-loads

Gravity split by certainty

ASCE 7 lumps everything permanent into D. ASCE 37 splits construction loads by how well you know them, because a load you can weigh deserves a smaller factor than a stockpile that grows when nobody's looking.

\(C_D\)Construction dead loadSelf-weight of temporary structures once assembled (erected scaffold, shoring).
\(C_{FML}\)Fixed material loadMaterials fixed in magnitude once placed, such as installed formwork.
\(C_{VML}\)Variable material loadStockpiles, and wet concrete until it cures.
\(C_P\)Personnel & equipmentUniform or concentrated, whichever is worse.
\(C_H\)Horizontal constructionBraking, personnel push, equipment; ≥ 2% of total vertical.
\(C_R\)Equipment reactionsOutrigger pads, crane tracks, hoists, pump boom.
\(C_C\)Lateral concrete pressureFresh concrete on vertical forms, per ACI 347 (falsework page).
\(C_F\)Erection & fitting forcesJacking, drifting or pulling members into alignment.
$$\text{ASCE 37-02 strength example: }\ 1.2D+1.2C_D+1.2C_{FML}+1.4C_{VML}+1.3W+0.5C_P+0.5L$$ $$\text{ASD structure (§2.3.1): }\ D+C_D+C_{FML}+C_{VML}\ ;\ \ \ldots+C_P+C_H+L\ ;\ \ \ldots+W+C_P+L\ ;\ \ \ldots+0.7E+C_P+L\ ;\ \ D+C_D+(W\ \text{or}\ 0.7E)$$Strength factors are in ASCE 37-14 Table 2-1 (verify the current values; the 37-02 form above shows the logic: CFML 1.2 vs CVML 1.4). The ASD combination structure is as summarized by ASEE (2008).
How the factors behave

\(C_D\) and \(C_{FML}\) behave like dead load. \(C_{VML}\) gets a higher factor because it's uncertain. \(C_P\) and \(C_H\) behave like live load. Only personnel and equipment loads may be reduced for large areas. \(C_H\) is not combined with W or E.
