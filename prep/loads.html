ASCE 37 doesn't replace ASCE 7. It takes the same analysis procedures, renames gravity loads by how certain they are, adds horizontal construction loads, and lowers the environmental loads to match a short exposure period. The pencil notes below mark what's new compared with your ASCE 7 course.

Combinations you already know

From your ASCE 7 course

The basic combinations are unchanged from ASCE 7-10 to 7-16 (W is already strength-level, so the factor is 1.0, or 0.6 in ASD). For temporary work, two of them do most of the work: the gravity case (1.2D + 1.6L…) sizes members, and the counteracting-dead-load case (0.9D + 1.0W, ASD 0.6D + 0.6W) checks overturning, uplift and sliding of towers, forms and mats.

ASCE 7-22 note

ASCE 7-22 changed the ground snow maps to strength level and dropped the LRFD snow factor to 1.0. It also moved Kd out of the velocity-pressure equation and into the pressure equations. ASCE 37-14 still points to ASCE 7-10, so the construction checks use 1.6S and qz = 0.00256 KzKztKdV². If they ask which edition you'd use, say so.

ASCE 37 vocabulary · Ch. 4
the C-loads

Gravity split by certainty

ASCE 7 lumps everything permanent into D. ASCE 37 splits construction loads by how well you know them, because a load you can weigh deserves a smaller factor than a stockpile that grows when nobody's looking.

CDConstruction dead loadSelf-weight of temporary structures once they're assembled, such as erected scaffold or shoring.
CFMLFixed material loadMaterials fixed in magnitude once placed, such as installed formwork.
CVMLVariable material loadMagnitude changes during work: stockpiles, and wet concrete until it cures.
CPPersonnel & equipmentCrew, hand tools, buggies. Uniform or concentrated, whichever is worse.
CHHorizontal constructionBraking, personnel push, equipment. Minimum 2% of total vertical.
CREquipment reactionsOutrigger pads, crane tracks, hoist reactions, concrete pump boom.
CCLateral concrete pressureFresh concrete on vertical forms. ASCE 37 defers to ACI 347 (see the falsework sheet).
CFErection & fitting forcesJacking, drifting or pulling members into alignment.
How the factors behave · Table 2-1

CD and CFML behave like dead load. CVML gets a higher factor than CFML because it's uncertain. ASCE 37-02 wrote it as 1.2CFML + 1.4CVML. CP and CH behave like live load. Only personnel and equipment loads may be reduced for large areas. Material loads never are.

Verify before quoting

Check the exact 37-14 factors and companion (arbitrary-point-in-time) values in Table 2-1 and Sec. 2.2–2.3 of a licensed copy. Explaining the logic matters more in an interview than reciting a decimal.

Personnel & equipment · Sec. 4.3
CP

Replace "occupancy live load"

There's no occupancy during construction, so the ASCE 7 Table 4-1 live loads don't fit. ASCE 37 classifies the operation on the deck instead, and pairs each uniform load with concentrated loads you check separately.

CP = 25 psfon a 20 × 30 ft bay: 15.0 kips

250 lb

Concentrated load per person. Check it on plywood, joists and walkway planks.

500 lb / wheel

Manually powered vehicles such as carts and hand buggies.

2,000 lb / wheel

Powered equipment. A motorized buggy on a plywood deck is a punching and local-bending check.

Interview angle

"Uniform or concentrated, whichever produces the more severe effect." The concentrated loads govern short-span sheathing and joists; the uniform loads govern stringers, caps and towers. Then compare with the owner spec. Caltrans falsework uses 20 psf live plus 75 plf at the deck edge, with a 100 psf minimum total. ACI 347 formwork uses 50 psf live (75 with motorized carts) and a 100 psf minimum total (125 with carts).

Verify

The light, medium and heavy psf values are as I recall them from Table 4-1 of 37-14. The concentrated values match published study notes. Confirm both against the standard.

Horizontal construction load · Sec. 4.4
CH

The 2% family

Real platforms are never perfectly plumb, and people and machines push things. ASCE 37 lists four sources of horizontal load. You design for the governing one and never go below 2% of the total vertical load. CH isn't combined with wind or earthquake. Run those as separate cases.

Will the gang form stand?

A classic first-week task: a free-standing wall form (or precast panel, or sign falsework) before the pour. Find the ASCE 37 wind, compare overturning with the stabilizing weight using the counteracting-dead-load combination, and size the push-pull braces.

Anchor uplift—kips / brace

Wind can come from either side, so the braces work in tension and compression. Size them as columns (push-pull pipe braces), and remember the deadman or anchor sees the vertical component. Kz is evaluated at the form top, which is conservative. G = 0.85, Kd = 0.85, Kzt = 1.0.

and always check the "unloaded & no concrete yet" stage. that's when forms blow over.
