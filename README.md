# ASCE 37 Wind & Snow + Temporary Works Interview Prep

Interactive study pages for a temporary-structures engineering interview.

- `index.html`: ASCE 37-14 construction-period wind factors, the hurricane-coast option, and snow.
- `prep/`: the full study set, with animated diagrams and calculators.
  - `prep/index.html`: hub with the role-to-topic map, a 7-day plan, the reading list, the temporary-works lifecycle, and a mixed flashcard drill.
  - `prep/loads.html`: ASCE 7 combinations vs. ASCE 37 construction loads, personnel classes, horizontal construction loads, and a wall-form wind check.
  - `prep/steel.html`: AISC column curve, LTB, Appendix 6 bracing, interaction with B1, and bolts and welds.
  - `prep/lifting.html`: sling geometry and CG, spreader vs. lifting beam, BTH-1 padeye, % of chart, crawler track pressure, pick points, and power-line clearance.
  - `prep/bridges.html`: AASHTO LRFD limit states, construction load factors, HL-93, a crane on a pile-bent trestle, stream flow, and twin-girder system buckling.
  - `prep/falsework.html`: ACI 347 form pressure, Caltrans falsework loads, the load path, an NDS joist check, and reshoring.
  - `prep/ground.html`: OSHA trench rules, Rankine + Boussinesq surcharge, Peck envelopes, a tremie seal, 2:1 spread, and barge stability.

Open the pages in a browser. Google Fonts load when a network connection is available; otherwise the pages fall back to system fonts.

This is an educational aid. Values marked "verify" come from memory or secondary sources. Check every provision against the current licensed standards, the project specification, and the authority having jurisdiction before using it in design.
